How I build your website

Six steps from the first call to a live site. I check every detail myself at every step, not only at the end. You see real pages early and you always know what comes next.

Keep scrolling →

  1. Step 1

    Free call

    A short call about your business: what's not working, who your customers are, and what the website should help with. No preparation needed.

  2. Step 2

    Plan the visitor's path

    I map what visitors see first, what makes them trust you and where they act. You get the page list, what each page does, a clear price and a timeline.

  3. Step 3

    Design

    I design real pages you can open on your own phone, not pictures of pages. You give feedback and we shape them together.

  4. Step 4

    Build

    Built with AI and checked by hand: the words, the images, the features and every detail in between.

  5. Step 5

    Test

    Every page on real phone sizes, page speed, forms, links and buttons. Nothing goes live until it all works.

  6. Step 6

    Launch and after

    Your domain connected, tracking checked, and a walkthrough of how to update your site. I stay on for fixes and changes.

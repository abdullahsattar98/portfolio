From ads to websites

How a marketer ended up building the part of the funnel he used to watch fail.

  1. Jan 2024

    Started in performance marketing

    I joined a digital marketing agency and ran Meta ads for businesses across different industries. My job was simple to say and hard to do: bring the right people in, for less.

  2. 2024 to 2025

    30+ ad accounts, $30K+ in spend

    I managed more than 30 ad accounts. For a tourism company in Dubai, I brought the cost of a lead under $1. Most days I was watching numbers: where people click, where they stop scrolling, where they leave.

  3. What I kept seeing

    The ads worked. The pages after them didn't.

    People clicked, then left on slow pages, confusing menus and forms nobody wanted to fill. The money was spent and the customer was gone. The click is only half the job.

  4. Jul 2025

    Started building websites with AI

    AI was changing marketing fast, so I didn't wait. I learned to design and build with it, and started working on the other half: the place where a visitor decides to stay or go.

  5. Today

    Live sites for real businesses

    NXT Tours, a Dubai tour company, runs on a booking site and admin panel I built. I also build concept projects like DHAKOO and NOIRE to try new ideas, like real-time 3D, before they go into client work.
